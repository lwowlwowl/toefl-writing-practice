# Discussion 001: Technology in Education

## Topic

### Professor's Question

**Dr. Martinez:**

Today we're discussing the role of technology in education. Some educators argue that laptops and tablets should be required in all university classrooms, as they enhance learning through instant access to information and interactive tools. Others believe that these devices are more distracting than helpful and that traditional note-taking methods lead to deeper understanding. What is your opinion? Should universities require students to use digital devices in classrooms, or should they encourage traditional learning methods?

### Student A (Claire)

I think universities should definitely require digital devices. We live in a technology-driven world, and students need to be comfortable using these tools. When I use my laptop in class, I can quickly look up unfamiliar concepts, access online textbooks, and collaborate with classmates through shared documents. Digital literacy is essential for the modern workplace, so using technology in class prepares students for their careers.

### Student B (Tomás)

I disagree. In my experience, having a laptop open during lectures is incredibly distracting. I end up checking social media or browsing the internet instead of paying attention. Research has shown that students who take handwritten notes retain information better because the physical act of writing forces them to process and summarize what they hear. I think banning devices would actually improve learning outcomes.
